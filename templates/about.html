Our Story

Built by students who lived the problem

PolytechEDGE began when a group of polytechnic graduates noticed how hard it was to find reliable study materials, clear career direction, and honest admission guidance — all in one place. Notes were scattered across groups, cutoff data was buried in PDFs, and career advice rarely spoke to diploma students specifically.

Instead of waiting for someone else to fix it, we started building. We gathered resources for K-Scheme and I-Scheme courses, created tools to help students predict college options, and wrote guides for polytechnic and DSE admissions. Today, PolytechEDGE continues to grow as we add more materials, refine our predictors, and listen to what students actually need.

"Every polytechnic student deserves access to quality resources and guidance — regardless of their background or location."
  • Started from real challenges faced during polytechnic education
  • Focused on Maharashtra polytechnic students and DTE admission cycles
  • Free platform — no paywall between students and essential resources

What We Do

Tools and resources for every stage

PolytechEDGE brings together the things polytechnic students need most — from semester notes to admission planning and career direction.

Polytech Portal

Study materials, notes, previous year papers, and manuals organized by branch and semester across K-Scheme and I-Scheme.

College Predictor

Personalized college recommendations based on your scores, preferences, and reservation category using historical DTE Maharashtra cutoff data.

Career Guidance

Higher education paths, course selection advice, and structured guidance to help you plan what comes after your diploma.

Job Roadmap & Admissions

Step-by-step job roadmaps for roles like full-stack development, plus guides for first-year polytechnic and DSE (diploma to B.Tech) admissions.

Our Mission

To give every polytechnic student free access to organized study resources, honest admission guidance, and practical career tools — so they can make informed decisions about their education and professional future without barriers.

Our Vision

A future where diploma education is taken seriously — where students graduate with clear direction, strong fundamentals, and direct paths into further study or meaningful work. We want PolytechEDGE to be the first place Maharashtra polytechnic students turn to for reliable help.

Our Process

How We Work

Everything we build starts with understanding what students actually struggle with.

Understand

We listen to students — their admission worries, study gaps, and career questions — before deciding what to build next.

Build

We turn those needs into practical tools: organized resource libraries, predictors, guides, and roadmaps that work on any device.

Learn

We gather feedback from students and educators, review how our tools perform in real admission cycles, and improve what is not working.

Grow

We expand coverage — more branches, more semesters, more career paths — so the platform stays useful as students' needs evolve.
